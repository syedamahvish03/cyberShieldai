What Gmail protection does

✓ Checks new incoming messages on a schedule

✓ Uses the existing CyberShield AI email detection model

✓ Shows suspicious results on your dashboard

✓ Sends an email alert when a threat is detected

✓ Does not scan historical messages or attachments

Privacy and data retention: Message content is used temporarily for analysis and is not saved in Gmail detection history. We retain only the sender, subject, received date, detection result and reasons, and URL domain names. Gmail detection history is automatically deleted after 30 days. Your separate manual-analysis history is not affected.

Scheduled checks can sometimes be delayed. This is periodic monitoring, not instant or guaranteed 24/7 protection.
